Mohammed Emon

Early-Stage Researcher · MSc in Marine Biotechnology

Computational structural biologist working at the intersection of biophysical simulation, cheminformatics, and machine learning. Completed the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master’s (EU-CONEXUS alliance) at UCV, La Rochelle Université, and AUA, with research visits at CNRS-LIENSs.

My work focuses on resolving target conformational plasticity and overcoming false-positive enrichment in virtual screening through automated Python/RDKit pipelines, ensemble docking (GNINA CNN scoring), all-atom molecular dynamics (GROMACS/Desmond), and MM-GBSA binding free-energy landscapes.

Methodological Workflow

End-to-End In Silico Therapeutics Pipeline

STEP 01
Library Standardization
Tautomer enumeration, chiral centers, macro-pKa curation, and 3D minimization.
STEP 02
Ensemble Screening
High-throughput docking across AlphaFold-refined & MD-derived receptor snapshots.
STEP 03
Consensus Ranking
Integration of GNINA CNN scoring, pharmacophore fit, and PLIF interaction profiles.
STEP 04
Free-Energy Decomposition
All-atom explicit solvent MD simulations and MM-GBSA validation for lead selection.
